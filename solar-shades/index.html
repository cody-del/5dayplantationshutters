Home/Shades/Solar shades

Find your balance

Screen openness balances
glare control and your view.

Solar shades use screen-like fabric that rolls up at the top. The weave influences both glare control and the view through the lowered shade.

A tighter weave

More coverage

A tighter screen weave generally limits more sunlight and reduces the view through the fabric. Compare a sample at the window rather than choosing from a small color image.

A more open weave

More view through

A more open screen generally preserves more of the outward view while allowing more light through. We’ll show the available openness choices for your selected fabric range.

Openness and color work together. Compare samples at your own window to judge the glare control and view you prefer.

Before you choose

Daytime views need
a nighttime privacy plan.

Solar screen fabric can be see-through after dark when the lights are on inside. For nighttime privacy, pair it with a suitable additional treatment or choose a more opaque fabric.

We can compare a more private roller fabric or discuss a complementary treatment. Tell us whether the window faces a street, another home, or an outdoor living area.

When is the glare strongest?
Tell us the time of day and where it lands: a television, desk, dining table, or seating area. That makes the fabric discussion more useful.
How much view do you want?
Look through the available samples at your glass. Fabric color and weave should be considered together.
How will you adjust the shade?
Compare offered manual or cordless controls, or rechargeable motorization where compatible with the product and dimensions.

Solar shade questions

Know what your screen fabric will do.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Are solar shades the same as blackout shades?

No. Solar screens are selected for glare control and some outward view. Blackout roller fabric serves a different light-blocking purpose, although light may still enter around its edges.

Can people see through solar shades at night?

They may be able to see into a lit room. We’ll discuss a different fabric or an additional treatment if you need nighttime privacy.

Which openness should I choose?

Compare screen samples at the window where you want less glare. A tighter weave generally limits more light and view; a more open weave preserves more view while letting more light through. We help you balance those priorities with the available fabrics.

Are your solar shades motorized?

Rechargeable motorized options are available where the selected system suits the fabric and dimensions. We confirm compatibility and controls before ordering.

What affects the cost of solar shades?

Size, fabric, controls, header choices, and installation affect the quote. Measurements and consultations are free.

Are solar shades made in your Largo workshop?

Our hybrid plantation shutters are manufactured in Largo. Our shade products are supplied by other manufacturers and installed by our in-house team.
