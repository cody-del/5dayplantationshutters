Start with the opening you use most

Choose a sliding-door treatment
around your daily access.

A treatment that looks right at a window may need a different configuration at a door. Tell us which side opens, where you walk, and how often you want access to the glass.

With vertical blinds, we discuss the direction of the draw and where the vanes gather. For shutters or shades, the available configuration depends on the product and the opening’s dimensions.

Explore vertical blinds for wide openings
Keep the route clear
We check handles, walking space, and nearby furniture before recommending how a treatment should open.
Compare windows beside the door
Different products can share a similar color or finish. See the samples together so the room feels coordinated even when the openings work differently.
Consider the controls
Manual, cordless, and rechargeable motorized shade options depend on the selected style and size. We confirm compatibility and show you how the controls work.

Choose the fabric in your light

A view in the day.
Privacy after dark.

A fabric that preserves an outward view during the day may allow views into a lit room at night. We’ll ask when you use each room and compare samples with those sightlines in mind.

Solar shades are worth discussing for glare and outward views. For bedrooms, compare more opaque roller shade or cellular shade fabrics and the light that can enter around the mounting edges.

For alternating sheer and solid bands, explore zebra shades. For natural woven texture, look at woven wood shades and available privacy liners.

St. Petersburg questions

St. Petersburg service questions, answered.

Call (813) 317-1077 to discuss your rooms and arrange a visit.

Do you measure and install in St. Petersburg?

Yes. We offer free in-home consultations and measurements in St. Petersburg. Our in-house team installs the products without subcontractors.

Do I have to choose one product for the whole home?

No. You can choose shutters, blinds, or shades room by room. We compare finishes together and account for the different light, privacy, and access needs of each opening.

Can you help with a sliding glass door?

Yes. We assess its dimensions, handles, direction of opening, and available clearance. Vertical blinds, shutters, and suitable shade configurations can be discussed, with options confirmed for the selected product.

Are your motorized shades rechargeable?

Yes. We offer rechargeable motorized shade options. Motor and control compatibility depend on the shade style and dimensions; we confirm those details before ordering.

Where can I visit you in person?

Our business is in Largo at 1876 Lake Ave SE, Unit F, Largo, FL 33771. Visits are by appointment. You can also have the free consultation at your St. Petersburg home.

What do I need to prepare for the consultation?

Tell us which rooms you want to update, what bothers you about the light or privacy, and any building requirements or access arrangements. Our team takes the measurements and discusses product choices, price, and expected timing.
