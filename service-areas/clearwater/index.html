The surface you’ll see every day

Hybrid shutters have a wood structure with a protective vinyl surface. PVC uses reinforced solid polymer construction. Hardwood can be painted or stained. Compare samples with your trim and nearby furnishings to decide which finish feels right.

The view between the louvers

Hybrid louvers come in 3″ and 4″ sizes; PVC in 2½″, 3½″, and 4½″; hardwood in 3½″ and 4″. Larger louvers create fewer horizontal lines across the same opening. We also check the space they need to tilt past handles and glass.

The way the panels open

A sofa, bedside table, or window handle can affect panel clearance. Tell us whether you want to open the panels often or mainly tilt the louvers. We’ll compare frame and panel configurations for the selected product.

Light when the shutters are closed

Louvers adjust daylight and sightlines, but light can still enter around panels and edges. If a darker bedroom is your priority, compare room-darkening roller shade fabrics alongside shutters.

Clearwater shutter questions

Clearwater shutter questions, answered.

Talk through your windows with us at (813) 317-1077.

Do you offer free shutter estimates in Clearwater?

Yes. We offer free in-home consultations and measurements in Clearwater. Our in-house team handles installation.

Where is your business located?

Our location is in Largo at 1876 Lake Ave SE, Unit F, Largo, FL 33771. You can visit by appointment or have us come to your Clearwater home.

Which shutter materials can I compare?

We offer hybrid shutters manufactured in Largo, plus supplier-made PVC and hardwood shutters. Hardwood can be painted or stained, and PVC has painted and custom color options. Current finishes are confirmed for your order.

Can you help choose shutters for a condo?

Yes. We can measure the openings and discuss the fit, appearance, and privacy you want. Share your building’s requirements and access arrangements before the appointment.

Can I get shutters in exactly five days?

Our name reflects a focus on fast turnaround, not a guaranteed five-day completion time. We discuss the expected schedule for your selected product and order.

What if a shade works better in one room?

You can mix treatments across your home. We offer faux wood and vertical blinds, plus roller, solar, zebra, cellular, and woven wood shades. We’ll compare the options for each opening.
