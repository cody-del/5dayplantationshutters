A bedroom with too much light

Compare light-filtering and more opaque fabrics in roller or cellular shades. The fabric limits light through the material; the mounting and edge coverage affect how dark the room becomes.

A living room where you want a view

Shutter louvers and faux wood blind slats tilt to adjust the light. Compare their spacing, the surrounding frame or headrail, and how the treatment looks alongside nearby windows.

A desk or television with glare

Tell us when sunlight crosses the screen. Compare the adjustment of tilting louvers with shade fabrics, including solar screen material. Discuss nighttime privacy before choosing a fabric that preserves an outward view.

A sliding door used throughout the day

Check access before matching its treatment to nearby windows. We compare vertical blinds and suitable shutter or shade configurations with handles, draw direction, and gathering space in mind.

Can I start with just one room in Brandon?

Yes. You can discuss one room or several during your free in-home consultation. Tell us which openings you want measured and which changes matter most.

Can I use different treatments in adjoining rooms?

Yes. We compare colors, finishes, and profiles together so different treatments can work alongside one another. Each opening is measured for its own fit and operation.

What affects the price of a whole-home order?

The number and size of openings, product selections, finishes, controls, and installation details affect the quote. We explain the options and what is included before you decide.

What should I have ready for the consultation?

A list of the rooms or openings, your main light and privacy concerns, and any building requirements is helpful. You don’t need to know the exact product or have precise measurements.
