Another option for a different room

Compare shutters
with fabric shades.

Shutters let you tilt louvers. Shades change light through fabric, with different choices for glare, privacy, texture, and room darkening.

Solar shades can soften glare while preserving some outward view. They can be see-through at night when the room is lit. For stronger privacy or a darker bedroom, compare more opaque roller fabrics and discuss light around the mounting edges.

For a sliding door, we can also compare vertical blinds with suitable shutter or shade configurations. Access and gathering space are part of that choice.

Which louver size leaves the most open view?

Larger louvers create fewer horizontal lines across the same window height, but the frame and panel layout also affect the view. Compare samples at your window and check the clearance needed for the chosen size.

Will shutters make a bedroom completely dark?

Shutters limit light when closed, but light can still enter around panels and edges. For a darker room, compare room-darkening shade fabrics and the mounting approach as well.

Can I use solar shades for nighttime privacy?

Solar screen fabrics can allow views into a lit room at night. If nighttime privacy is a priority, discuss a more opaque fabric or another treatment during your consultation.
