Zebra shades use alternating sheer and more opaque horizontal bands in two layers. As those layers move, the bands line up differently to change the view and light through the shade.

During your consultation, compare the fabric in its open and closed alignments. The horizontal pattern is part of the room’s look even when the shade is lowered.

Layered shade questions

Two names. One distinctive style.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Are zebra and layered shades different products?

We use both names for the same category: shades with alternating sheer and more opaque horizontal fabric bands. You can compare the available fabrics at your consultation.

Do I have to raise the whole shade to adjust the light?

Changing the band alignment lets you adjust the light and view while the shade is lowered. The operating system controls that movement and also raises the treatment.

Will the closed bands give me complete blackout?

Closed bands provide more coverage, but blackout depends on fabric opacity, overlap, and edge gaps. For stronger room darkening, compare more opaque roller shade fabrics too.

Can layered shades be motorized?

Rechargeable motorization is available for compatible options. We confirm the chosen shade, window size, motor, and controls together.

Can I choose the same fabric for different window sizes?

We’ll check what the selected range permits and explain any configuration differences. Each opening is measured individually.

Who measures and installs the shades?

Our in-house team handles both. Consultations and measurements are free across Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.
