Frequently asked questions

Your window treatment questions, answered.

Have a question about your windows? Give us a call. We’re happy to help.

Where are your plantation shutters manufactured?

We manufacture hybrid shutters in Largo. We also offer PVC and hardwood shutters made by our manufacturing partners. Our in-house team measures and installs throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.

Can I get an estimate at my home?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a free in-home estimate. We can discuss your windows, product preferences, measurements, and installation needs.

How long will my order take?

We’ll confirm the current production and installation schedule for your project. Timing depends on your product, measurements, and order details.

Do you also offer shades and blinds?

Yes. Alongside plantation shutters, we offer blinds and shades. Your consultation is a good time to compare the options for each room.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Pinellas and Hillsborough counties, including Largo, Clearwater, St. Petersburg, Tampa, Brandon, and surrounding communities. Call us to discuss your location and arrange an estimate.

What types of shades can I choose from?

Our shade options include roller, solar, zebra (layered), cellular (honeycomb), and woven wood shades. We can discuss your light and privacy preferences during your consultation.

What types of blinds do you offer?

We offer faux wood horizontal blinds and vertical blinds. We’ll help you compare options for your windows and doors.

Can you help me choose window treatments?

Yes. We’ll talk through the options for your space, including how you want to control natural light and privacy, and help you choose shutters, blinds, or shades for each room.
