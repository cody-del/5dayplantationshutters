Choose the material as well as the mechanism

The controls move it.
The material shapes the light.

Motorization changes how you operate a shade. Its fabric, lining, and mounting still determine the light and privacy.

A simple fabric surface

Roller shades can use light-filtering or more opaque fabric. For a darker room, discuss both the fabric and light around the edges of the installation.

Glare and an outward view

Solar screen fabric can soften glare while retaining some view outside. It may allow views into a lit room at night, so compare it against your privacy needs.

A layered or pleated look

Zebra shades use alternating sheer and solid bands. Cellular shades have honeycomb-shaped pockets. Compare their appearance, opacity, and available controls.

Wood texture or a shutter finish

Woven wood shades bring natural texture, with privacy depending on the weave and liner. For a framed finish, compare hybrid, PVC, and hardwood plantation shutters.

Can I compare motorized shades at my Riverview consultation?

Yes. We discuss rechargeable options and confirm which motors and controls are compatible with the shade style and dimensions you choose.

Are cordless and motorized shades the same thing?

No. A cordless lift is a manual way of moving a shade without a hanging lift cord. A motorized shade uses a powered system. Available options depend on the selected product and opening.

Can every shade style be motorized?

Compatibility depends on the shade, fabric, dimensions, and operating system. We check the available combination for your order before recommending it.

How often will a rechargeable shade need charging?

The interval depends on the selected motor, shade, and use. We explain the manufacturer’s charging instructions for your system rather than promising one interval for every shade.

Do you also install shutters and blinds in Riverview?

Yes. We offer hybrid, PVC, and hardwood plantation shutters, faux wood horizontal blinds, vertical blinds, and five shade styles. Our in-house team handles measuring and installation.

Can the consultation take place at my home?

Yes. Consultations and measurements are free in Riverview. Our business is based in Largo, where visits are available by appointment; you can compare the products at home without traveling there.
