A practical way to work

One in-house team.
From measurements to installation.

Our Largo business does more than sell window treatments. We manufacture hybrid plantation shutters in-house and take responsibility for measuring and installing the products we sell.

You can compare our locally made hybrid shutters with PVC and hardwood shutters from manufacturing partners, or choose blinds and shades for other rooms. Your measuring and installation stay with our in-house team; we don’t use subcontractors.

Free consultation and measurements

We visit homes throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties. Compare options in your own rooms and let us check dimensions, trim, handles, and mounting clearance.

Local hybrid manufacturing

Our hybrid shutter line combines a wood structure with a protective vinyl surface. We manufacture this line in Largo; other product lines come from their manufacturers.

Timing for your chosen product

“5 Day” reflects our focus on fast turnaround. We discuss production and installation timing for your specific order before you decide; it is not a guaranteed five-day completion date.

Know the coverage before ordering

Our plantation shutters carry a limited lifetime warranty. Ask for the written coverage, exclusions, and applicable terms. Shade, blind, and motor coverage depends on the selected product.
