Motorized shade questions

Rechargeable motorized shade questions, answered.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Do the motors use rechargeable batteries?

Yes. The motorized shade options we offer use rechargeable batteries. We explain the charging method for the selected system.

How often do I need to recharge them?

The interval depends on the motor, shade size and weight, and how often you use it. Follow the charging instructions for your selected system and keep its charging point accessible.

Can I stop a shade partway up?

A compatible remote includes a Stop control. We demonstrate operation and the configured travel limits when your shades are installed. The interactive example above lets you try Up, Stop, and Down.

Does every shade style support the same motor?

No. Compatibility depends on the product, dimensions, fabric, and configuration. We check that combination before you order.

Are app and voice controls included?

Those features depend on the selected system and any required accessories. Tell us what you want to connect so we can confirm compatibility and the complete quote.

Who installs and sets up the shades?

Our in-house installation team handles your order. We measure the openings, explain the selected controls, and install throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.
