Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 5 DAY PLANTATION SHUTTERS TAMPA BAY LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Consultation details
  • IP address, browser/device info (for analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, arrange consultations, prepare estimates, and coordinate orders and installation. We may also request feedback and improve our services. Promotional communications require any applicable consent.

The consultation form prepares an email in your own email app. Preparing that draft does not deliver a request to us; you must send the email. If you send it, we receive the information included in the message and use it to respond.

3. SMS Consent & Communication

Providing a phone number in a consultation request does not, by itself, subscribe you to promotional texts. Promotional SMS requires a separate opt-in; it is not a condition of purchasing window treatments.

If you choose SMS communications, the opt-in describes the messages you agree to receive and their frequency. Message and data rates may apply. You can withdraw consent by replying STOP to a message or contacting us at 5dayshutters@gmail.com or (813) 317-1077.

4. Data Sharing

Trusted service providers, such as hosting, email, and customer-management providers, may process information to provide services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties for their own promotional marketing.

5. Data Security

We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no system is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at 5dayshutters@gmail.com.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies for analytics and functionality. You can manage your preferences via your browser settings.

8. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. We encourage users to review it regularly.
