The surface you’ll see every day

Hybrid shutters have a wood structure with a protective vinyl surface. PVC uses reinforced solid polymer construction. Hardwood can be painted or stained. Compare samples with your trim and nearby furnishings to decide which finish feels right.

The view between the louvers

Hybrid louvers come in 3″ and 4″ sizes; PVC in 2½″, 3½″, and 4½″; hardwood in 3½″ and 4″. Larger louvers create fewer horizontal lines across the same opening. We also check the space they need to tilt past handles and glass.

The way the panels open

A sofa, bedside table, or window handle can affect panel clearance. Tell us whether you want to open the panels often or mainly tilt the louvers. We’ll compare frame and panel configurations for the selected product.

Light when the shutters are closed

Louvers adjust daylight and sightlines, but light can still enter around panels and edges. If a darker bedroom is your priority, compare room-darkening roller shade fabrics alongside shutters.
