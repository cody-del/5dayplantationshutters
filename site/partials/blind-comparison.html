Faux wood or vertical blinds?
StyleHow it movesFit to check
Faux wood horizontalSlats tilt for light control and lift together for an open viewSlat clearance, reachable controls, and space for the raised blind above the glass
VerticalVanes tilt for light control and draw sideways for accessDoor opening direction, handles, and room for the vanes to gather beside the glass

Both adjust light while lowered or drawn across the opening. Compare the closed position for privacy and the open position for your view and everyday access.
