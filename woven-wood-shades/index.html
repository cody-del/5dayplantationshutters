Compare the weave
and the privacy behind it.

Woven wood shades bring natural material texture and variation to a room. The weave’s openness changes how much light and view pass through it.

The visible surface

Compare real samples

Hold the available weaves against your flooring, furnishings, and wall colors. Natural variation is part of the appearance. Compare each sample’s material and weave alongside your room finishes.

The room behind it

Plan the privacy

An open weave can leave a view through the shade. If privacy or a darker room matters, discuss available lining options and how they work with the selected product.

A useful conversation at your window

Choose the look.
Check it in your light.

The same weave can look different against bright glass than it does on a table. Tell us whether your priority is decorative texture, soft daylight, or privacy.

We’ll also discuss available manual, cordless, and compatible rechargeable motorized controls. Those choices depend on the selected weave, configuration, and opening.

Bedroom or living area?
Start with the room’s privacy and light requirements. An unlined decorative weave may not meet a bedroom’s needs.
Mounting and clearance
We measure the opening, check obstructions, and discuss where the shade will sit and gather when raised.
Care before purchase
Confirm the selected weave’s care guidance and suitability for the room, especially if moisture or frequent splashes are a concern.

Woven shade questions

Woven wood shade questions, answered.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Are woven wood shades solid wood blinds?

No. Woven shades use woven material rather than tilting solid slats. Our horizontal blind offering is faux wood.

Can people see through the weave?

That depends on its openness and the lighting. Compare the sample and discuss a lining option if privacy is important.

Does every woven shade have a blackout liner?

Liner choices vary by product. If a darker bedroom is the goal, compare the available liner, its light-blocking properties, and coverage around the installed shade.

Can woven shades be rechargeable and motorized?

Compatible motorized options are available. We confirm the motor and configuration for your chosen shade and opening.

Will every shade have exactly the same natural pattern?

Natural materials have variation. Compare samples and discuss the expected appearance across neighboring windows before ordering.

Do you measure and install woven shades?

Yes. Measurements and consultations are free, and our own team installs the finished products across Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.
