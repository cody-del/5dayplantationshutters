Look inside the fabric

Honeycomb pockets add
an insulating layer.

Cellular shades form honeycomb-shaped pockets that hold air, adding an insulating layer at the window. “Cellular” and “honeycomb” describe this same shade category.

Daylight you can live with

Light-filtering fabrics

Compare the available fabrics for soft daylight in rooms you use during the day. Look at color, visible texture, and privacy together.

For a darker room

Darkening options

If bedrooms or screen glare are your concern, ask about the available darker fabrics. Mounting and light around the treatment still affect the room’s result.

Compare the available cell configurations and fabric opacity at your consultation. The insulating effect also depends on the window, fit, and conditions in your home.

Consider the daily routine

Cordless or rechargeable?
Start with your reach.

Tell us which shades you’ll adjust every morning, which sit behind furniture, and which windows are difficult to reach. We’ll compare the offered controls with your routine.

A cordless option can suit reachable windows where available. Compatible rechargeable motorization lets you adjust a shade with a control instead of reaching the window.

Clearance at the opening
We measure the window and check trim, handles, and mounting depth before recommending the position.
Shade raised
The gathered fabric needs room at the top. We discuss how the selected configuration will sit relative to the visible glass.
Care and coverage
Ask for the selected fabric’s cleaning instructions and written shade or motor warranty. Those terms depend on the products ordered.

Cellular shade questions

A few things to know about honeycomb.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Is honeycomb another name for cellular shades?

Yes. The names refer to the same product category and the pockets formed inside the fabric.

Will cellular shades lower my energy bill?

Cellular pockets add insulation at the window. The effect on energy use depends on your glass, shade fit, climate, and heating or cooling habits; there is no single savings figure for every home.

Can I choose a darker fabric for a bedroom?

We’ll compare the available room-darkening options and the mounting position. Light around the sides and top can still enter the room.

Do you offer rechargeable motors?

Yes, for compatible cellular options. Availability depends on the selected shade and dimensions, and we confirm the system before ordering.

Can every window have the same control?

Window size, reach, and available configurations guide the control choice. Reachable windows may suit hand operation; higher windows are worth comparing with compatible rechargeable motorization.

Can I compare cellular shades with roller shades?

Yes. Roller shades have a different fabric profile and roll at the top. We can compare both styles in the same free consultation.
