The consultation comes to you

A free consultation at
your Hillsborough home.

You don’t need to choose your window treatments from a screen or travel to Largo for the first conversation. We offer free in-home consultations and measurements throughout Hillsborough County.

Show us the rooms you want to update, tell us what isn’t working with the current treatments, and compare options for the way you use your home. Our own team handles installation.

Arrange an in-home consultation
Tell us the address
Include your city and the property address when scheduling. If access requires a gate, parking instructions, or building arrangements, let us know.
Walk through the rooms
We look at mounting space, glass coverage, reach, and clearance. Mention strong glare, screen reflections, or privacy concerns as we compare products.
Confirm the order
We discuss the selected configuration, quote, and expected schedule. Installation is handled by our in-house team, with no subcontractors.

Make the appointment useful

Glare, privacy, or a busy doorway?
Plan around each opening.

Several rooms, one update

List the openings you want measured. We can compare shutters in one room with blinds or shades elsewhere and discuss which rooms to prioritize.

Busy doors and wide openings

Show us how you move through sliding doors. Draw direction, stacking room, handles, and mounting space help determine the treatment choice.

Hard-to-reach windows

Ask about rechargeable motorized shades where compatible. We plan charging access and explain the selected controls.

Bedrooms and bright living areas

Compare fabric privacy and darkness with actual window fit. A solar fabric for daytime glare has different nighttime privacy behavior from a more opaque roller fabric.

Select the treatment before the finish

Explore shutters, blinds, and shades.

Our hybrid shutters are made in Largo. PVC and hardwood shutters, blinds, and shades are supplied by other manufacturers and installed by our team.

Plantation shutters

Choose locally made hybrid shutters for a protective vinyl surface, PVC for reinforced polymer construction, or hardwood for a painted or stained natural wood finish.

Blinds

For windows, compare faux wood slats that tilt and lift. For sliding doors, compare vertical vanes that tilt and draw sideways, with stacking space beside the opening.

Shades

Roller, solar, zebra, cellular, and woven wood styles. Compare fabrics for light, privacy, and the appearance you want.

Rechargeable motorization

Compatible powered shade options for convenient adjustment. We confirm the rechargeable motor and controls for the chosen product and dimensions.
