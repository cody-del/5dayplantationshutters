The details make the difference

Louver size, panel swing,
and the fit at your window.

How you use a room matters just as much as how it looks. We’ll work through these choices with you before you order.

Light and privacy

Tilt the louvers to adjust daylight and the view into your room. Discuss how much privacy you need, where glare is a problem, and whether you’ll want to open the panels regularly. Shutters are not a guaranteed blackout treatment.

Louver size and proportion

Louvers are the horizontal slats you tilt to control light. Our hybrid shutters come in 3″ and 4″ sizes, PVC in 2½″, 3½″ and 4½″, and hardwood in 3½″ and 4″. Compare samples against your window; we’ll check the clearance around handles and other obstructions.

Frames and mounting

Window depth, trim, and nearby furniture can affect the fit and the way a panel opens. Our measurements help determine the appropriate frame, mounting position, and panel arrangement.

Doors and unusual openings

Have a sliding glass door, French door, arch, or another unusual opening? We’ll assess its dimensions and operation, then explain the configurations available in the shutter line you choose.

Plantation shutter questions

Plantation shutter questions, answered.

Need help with a particular window? Call (813) 317-1077.

Which shutters do you manufacture locally?

We manufacture hybrid plantation shutters at our Largo workshop. Our PVC and hardwood shutters are made by our manufacturing partners and installed by our in-house team.

How do hybrid and PVC shutters differ?

Our hybrid shutters combine a wood structure with a protective vinyl surface. Our PVC shutters use solid polymer construction with aluminum reinforcement in the louvers and stiles. We can show you both materials during your consultation.

Can I choose a painted or stained finish?

Hardwood shutters are available painted or stained. PVC offers painted and custom color options. We’ll confirm the current finishes for your selected product before placing the order.

Do you use subcontractors to install shutters?

No. Our in-house team handles your measurements and installation throughout our service area.

Are in-home measurements and consultations free?

Yes. We offer free in-home consultations and measurements throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties, including Largo, Clearwater, St. Petersburg, Tampa, Brandon, and Riverview.

Can I visit the Largo location?

Yes, by appointment. Call (813) 317-1077 to arrange a visit to 1876 Lake Ave SE, Unit F, Largo, FL 33771.

Can you help with shutters for a sliding glass door?

Yes. We can assess your door, how you use it, and the space around it. Track configurations and other options depend on the selected shutter line and the opening’s measurements.

Do you offer blinds and shades as well?

Yes. We carry faux wood and vertical blinds, plus roller, solar, zebra, cellular, and woven wood shades. Explore our window treatments or compare options at your consultation.
