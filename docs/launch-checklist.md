# Rebuild and launch checklist

## Current draft

- Homepage HTML/CSS, asymmetric header corners, centered existing logo, mobile call/estimate bar.
- Reviews immediately after hero: links to the existing site's Google place ID. No invented ratings, counts, or review text.
- Root canonical preserved. Draft noindex remains until production deployment.
- Current publicly listed contact details and three established product lines are provisional, pending owner confirmation.
- No five-day completion, warranty, number-one ranking, years-in-business, or Fort Myers claims.

## Required before production

- Confirm address, phone, email, logo rights, photo rights, active products, service coverage, current turnaround and precise five-day promise.
- Owner supplies authentic Google reviews with author attribution and links. Verify the Google place ID against the actual GBP. Add excerpts below hero, with accessible mobile layout. Do not add self-serving LocalBusiness AggregateRating markup.
- Confirm actual customer-facing business hours and showroom access before publishing them or including them in schema.
- Migrate and improve existing product, county, About, gallery, contact and legal pages before switching the domain. Absolute links in this draft intentionally lead to the existing website, and must become local migrated routes at that point.
- Preserve /plantation-shutters, /roller-shades, /horizontal-blinds, /Pinellas-window-treatments, /Hillsborough-window-treatments, /about-5day, /gallary, /contact-5day, /privacy-policy and /terms-and-conditions. Do not rename /gallary just for spelling without a redirect.
- Export Search Console query/page history, current indexed URL inventory, analytics conversions and valuable backlinks. Verify cannibalization from switching URLs and intent overlap rather than repeated branded rows.
- Protect / as the main Tampa/Tampa Bay plantation-shutters entry point. Build product depth separately; avoid a competing Tampa shutter page until supported by evidence.
- Add useful Largo, Clearwater and St. Petersburg coverage with real projects, then expand to other served cities based on demand and evidence. Do not generate city-name-swapped duplicates.
- Connect and test the production contact flow (success, validation, failure, spam protection and lead delivery). Keep necessary existing consent/privacy behavior and validate integrations before replacement.
- Add accurate LocalBusiness/Organization entity data and breadcrumbs after details are confirmed, matching visible content. No fabricated reviews or unsupported product claims.
- Configure server-side 301 /home-7396 to /. Test real HTTP status and Location headers, canonical consistency, trailing-slash/case behavior, and every changed URL. Do not use blanket homepage redirects for removed service pages.
- Generate a sitemap containing only final canonical indexable pages. Validate robots.txt and remove the draft noindex only at production launch. Staging should remain excluded from indexing.
- Validate mobile layout, keyboard access, working call links, form delivery, Core Web Vitals, metadata, structured data and all internal links. Fonts currently load from Google Fonts; self-host selected WOFF2 files if desired before release.
- Track call clicks separately from actual calls, and successful form submissions separately from form starts. Compare Search Console landing-page performance and leads after launch.
