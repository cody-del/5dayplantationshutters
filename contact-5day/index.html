Before your consultation

A few details to help you plan.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Are the consultation and measurements free?

Yes. We offer free in-home consultations and measurements throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.

Do I need to know the exact product before contacting you?

No. Tell us about the room, light, privacy, and look you want. We can compare shutters, blinds, and shades together.

Can I arrange a visit to your Largo location?

Yes, by appointment. Call (813) 317-1077 before visiting 1876 Lake Ave SE, Unit F, Largo, FL 33771.

Who installs the products I choose?

Our in-house team handles measuring and installation. We don’t use subcontractors.
