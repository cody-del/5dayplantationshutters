Home/Blinds/Vertical blinds

Think about the opening

Tilt for light.
Draw sideways for access.

Vertical blinds have hanging vanes that tilt for light control and draw to the side. For a sliding glass door, that sideways movement can suit the way you enter and leave the room.

At your consultation, show us which panel opens, where you walk, and any furniture close to the glass. We’ll measure the opening and check where the vanes will gather when the blind is open.

Draw direction
We compare the offered draw configurations with the door’s opening side. The gathered vanes should sit where they interfere least with everyday access.
Handle and trim clearance
The treatment needs space to tilt and move without catching on handles or surrounding trim. Mounting position is part of the measurement.
The whole opening
Wide windows and doors need a quote based on their actual dimensions. We’ll confirm the available headrail, vane, finish, and operating choices for that size.

More than a color choice

Choose the vane finish, draw direction, and controls.

A sample helps you compare the visible surface. A discussion about daily use helps you choose how the blind should work.

Light and privacy

Tilting the vanes changes the view and incoming light. Check the closed position too; gaps between vanes and around the treatment affect privacy and room darkness.

Texture and color

Compare available vane samples with your walls, flooring, and other window treatments. We confirm material and care instructions for the product you select.

Comfortable controls

We explain the offered operating mechanism and check where you’ll reach it. Tell us if furniture or the door position makes one side easier to use.

Cost and timing

Dimensions, vane selection, headrail, controls, and installation details affect the estimate. Your quote includes the chosen configuration and expected order timing.

Vertical blind questions

Vertical blind questions, answered.

Have a particular window in mind? Call (813) 317-1077.

Do you measure sliding glass doors?

Yes. Our free in-home measurement includes the opening, mounting space, handles, and how you use the door.

Do vertical blinds have to open to one particular side?

The available draw configurations depend on the product. We’ll compare them with your door and confirm the chosen direction before ordering.

Will vertical blinds make the room completely dark?

Closed vanes reduce the light and view, but the treatment is not a sealed blackout system. If darkness is your main goal, discuss that before choosing the product.

Can I coordinate them with blinds on other windows?

Yes. We can compare finishes for your door and faux wood blinds elsewhere during the same consultation.

Should I compare shades for my sliding door?

Yes, where the opening permits. A shade usually needs to be raised clear for access. Compare roller shades and their mounting requirements with a blind that draws sideways.

Who installs my vertical blinds?

Our own installation team handles the work throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties. We don’t use subcontractors.
