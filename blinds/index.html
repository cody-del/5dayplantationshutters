Faux wood or vertical?
Compare the way they move.

We offer faux wood horizontal blinds and vertical blinds throughout Pinellas and Hillsborough counties. The shape of your opening and how you use it help guide the choice.

Faux wood or vertical blinds?
StyleHow it movesFit to check
Faux wood horizontalSlats tilt for light control and lift together for an open viewSlat clearance, reachable controls, and space for the raised blind above the glass
VerticalVanes tilt for light control and draw sideways for accessDoor opening direction, handles, and room for the vanes to gather beside the glass

Vertical blinds

Vertical blinds have hanging vanes that rotate to adjust light and draw to the side for access. They are an option for sliding glass doors and wide windows where side-to-side movement suits the opening.

Details worth comparing

Check light, mounting depth, and access.

We’ll help you work through the practical choices before you order.

Light and privacy

Think about street-facing windows, afternoon glare, and the times you use each room. Tilting slats or vanes changes the light and sightlines; small gaps can still let light through when they’re closed.

Inside or outside the frame

Mounting inside the window recess can leave more trim visible. Mounting outside extends beyond the opening. Window depth and obstructions help determine which approach fits.

Everyday access

Consider how often you open a window or use a patio door. We’ll check where a raised or drawn-back blind gathers and how that affects your view and access.

Finishes and controls

Compare finishes with your trim, flooring, and nearby furniture. We’ll explain the operating controls available for the product and size you choose, so you know how you’ll use it each day.

What types of blinds do you offer?

We offer faux wood horizontal blinds and vertical blinds. We’ll help you compare them based on the opening, the look you want, and how you need to control light and privacy.

Are faux wood blinds the same as wood blinds?

Faux wood blinds offer a wood look using manufactured materials rather than natural wood slats. Our current horizontal blind offering is faux wood. If you want a natural wood window treatment, you can also explore our hardwood plantation shutters.

Can vertical blinds work on sliding glass doors?

Yes, depending on the opening and available space. We check the door’s operation, handles, mounting position, and where the vanes gather when drawn back before recommending a configuration.
